About me

Hi, I'm Rebecca Ayele

I live in Abu Dhabi and build websites and apps from the ground up with AI. I trained in fashion design at Misrak TVET College and in graphic design at Dani Advertising and School of Graphics in Addis Ababa, so I care about how a page looks as much as how it works.

Before anything goes live, I check every line myself: the facts, the wording, the order of the steps, and the customer's privacy. Your customers only see work I'd put my own name on.

Besides client sites, I've built a private health app that keeps all its data on the phone, and a free 8-module course on UAE visa and PRO work, both in English and Amharic.

Questions

Things people usually ask

Do I need to know anything about computers?

No. You send me your photos, prices and a few words about your business on WhatsApp. I do the rest.

Can you make a website just for me, not a business?

Yes. A personal portfolio shows employers or clients who you are, what you've done and how to reach you. It's often more memorable than a CV on its own.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo if you have one, photos of your products or shop, your prices, your opening hours and your contact details. If you don't have good photos yet, I can tell you how to take them on your phone.

Will it be mine?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and you own the website. If you stop monthly care, I hand everything over to you.

How long does it take?

Most sites are live in about a week. It depends on how quickly you send photos and approve the design.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many pages you need and what the site should do. After a free chat I send you one clear price, with no hidden extras.

How do I pay?

Half when we start, half when the site goes live.
